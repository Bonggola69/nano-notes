    How the hybrid AI works

    Press Summarize and the app tries, in order:

    1. On-device — Gemini Nano in Chrome, via Firebase AI Logic. Private, free, works offline.
    2. Cloud — Gemini 2.5 Flash via Firebase AI Logic, used automatically for long documents or when the device model is unavailable.
    3. Extractive fallback — a local sentence extractor, clearly labelled, when no model is available at all. This is not AI output.

    The badge next to every summary tells you which engine produced it. Nothing is ever presented as AI when it is not.
